Pengyu

Privacy

Pengyu keeps your plan, workouts and Fish on your device. No account. No tracking. No ads.

Last updated November 1, 2026

The short version

  • Everything you do in Pengyu is saved in this browser, on this device.
  • Pengyu has no account, no server database and no analytics.
  • We never see your workouts. We don’t sell, share or rent any data.
  • You can export everything, or delete everything, at any time.

What Pengyu saves on your device

Pengyu keeps one record in your browser’s local storage, named pengyu.v1. It holds:

  • Your weekly plans: how many workouts, which days, a time, and your place.
  • Your workouts: the day and time you tapped Begin, planned or extra.
  • Your Fish: what you have now and everything you’ve earned.
  • Your unlocks: gear, train upgrades and extras, and what Pengu wears.
  • Comeback stamps, full-week bonuses and free covers.
  • A short diary of app moments, like a workout logged, each with a time.
  • Whether you’ve seen the Home Screen tip.

It also keeps a note that the opening splash already showed this session.

Files for offline use

To work offline, Pengyu stores its own app files in your browser. That means pages, styles, code, fonts and icons. They’re the same for everyone and hold nothing about you.

What Pengyu doesn’t collect

  • No name, email, phone number or password. There’s no account.
  • No location. Pengyu never asks for it.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • No body data: no weight, steps, heart rate, distance or pace.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads and no advertising ID.
  • No cookies. Pengyu doesn’t set any.
  • No code or fonts from other companies. Everything loads from Pengyu’s own site.

Who can see your data

Only you, on this device. It leaves only in a backup file you choose to save.

We don’t sell, share or rent data. We never receive it in the first place.

Netlify hosts Pengyu’s site. Loading any website shares basic request details with its host. For Netlify, that means details like your IP address and browser type. Netlify’s privacy statement explains how it handles them.

If you get Pengyu from an app store, it runs this same web app. The store and your browser have their own privacy policies.

Export or delete your data

  • Export my data (JSON): open History, then Your data, then Save a backup. You get a file named pengyu-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json with everything above.
  • Restore: Restore from a backup reads only the file you pick, on your device.
  • Delete everything: open History, then Your data, then Reset everything. Confirm twice.
  • Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings also deletes it all.
  • Backup and calendar files stay where you saved them. Delete them like any file.
  • Add reminders makes a calendar file on your device. Your calendar app has its own policy.
  • Saving a backup asks your browser to keep Pengyu’s storage. Some browsers ask you first.
