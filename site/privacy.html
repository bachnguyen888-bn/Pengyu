Pengyu

Privacy

Your plan, workouts and Fish stay in this browser. No account. No tracking. No ads.

Last updated November 11, 2026

The short version

  • Pengyu saves your plans and progress on this device, in this browser’s storage (localStorage). There’s no account or app server (backend).
  • The app does not send your plans, workout history or other health data to a server.
  • We do not sell your data. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking services.
  • You can download a backup of your saved app data, or reset it and start fresh.

What Pengyu saves on your device

Pengyu keeps your save in your browser’s local storage (localStorage). The record is named pengyu.v1. It holds:

  • Your weekly plans: how many workouts, which days, a time, the time-window and place text you enter, and any days you move.
  • Your workouts: the day and time you tapped Log finished workout, planned or extra.
  • Your Fish: what you have now and everything you’ve earned.
  • Your unlocks: gear, train upgrades and extras, what Pengu wears, and your train paint.
  • Records of returning after a planned workout was not logged (Comeback stamps), completing your weekly plan (full-week bonuses), and protecting an existing week streak through a week with workouts not logged or no plan made (free covers, once per calendar month).
  • A dated list of app actions, such as opening the app, making a plan, logging a workout or saving a backup. This list stays here; it is not sent to an analytics service.
  • When your save began, whether you’ve saved calendar reminders, and whether you’ve seen the Home Screen tip.

A temporary storage check is written and removed when the app starts. A separate temporary note (in sessionStorage) remembers that the opening splash already showed in this tab.

There’s no automatic cloud backup or sync between browsers. Another browser, or Pengyu added to your Home Screen, may have a separate save; a backup file carries progress between them. If browser storage is blocked, changes won’t survive leaving the app. Clearing site data also removes your save.

Files for offline use

To work offline, Pengyu keeps an offline copy (cache) of its own app files in your browser’s Cache Storage. That means pages, styles, code, fonts and icons, plus a short note of which version of those files is in use. They’re the same for everyone and hold nothing about you.

What Pengyu doesn’t collect

  • No name, email, phone number or password. There’s no account.
  • No device location access. The place text you choose to type is saved locally with your plan.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads and no advertising ID.
  • No cookies. Pengyu doesn’t set any.
  • No third-party code or font requests. The app’s code, fonts and icons load from the same site.

Who can see your data

Anyone with access to this browser profile can open Pengyu and see its saved data. Anyone you give a backup file to can read that file.

Loading or updating the app requests its files from the website host. Those requests do not include your saved plans or progress. The host may keep request logs, separate from your saved app data.

If you choose Download calendar reminders, Pengyu downloads a calendar file containing your planned days, times, time-window and place text. Importing that file into a calendar puts those details under that calendar’s settings. The reminders come from your calendar; Pengyu sends no notifications.

If you choose Preview your postcard, Pengyu draws the postcard image on your device: Pengu, your train and the prop you pick. Share passes that image and the text you picked to your device’s share sheet; where the share sheet can’t take images, only the text. The preview explains what Share will send before you tap it. Copy text copies the postcard text, and Download postcard saves the picture to your device. The postcard never includes a name or place. The dates, a workout count and the Pengyu link are added only if you choose them.

After your first plan, and any time in History, Pengyu offers an optional invitation for a friend. Share invitation or Copy invite passes along the words you see: the same short message for everyone, then the Pengyu link when it’s available. Your days, time or place are added only if you switch them on. Resetting or restoring a save clears these sharing choices.

The Pengyu link is https://bachnguyenclearhealth.com. It is offered only while you use Pengyu at that address; anywhere else, Pengyu says the link will work once this version is live. It has no tracking codes, and Pengyu never learns whether anyone opened it. Nothing is sent until you pick where it goes.

Look out the window shows three short, authored moments drawn on your device. It saves nothing and sends nothing.

Save a backup (JSON)

  • Open Settings → Your data → Save a backup. The same controls are also in History.
  • Your browser downloads pengyu-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json. This JSON file is a readable copy of your saved plans, progress, preferences and local action list, plus the export time. It does not include the splash flag or cached app files.
  • Restore: Restore from a backup reads only the file you pick, on your device. Files larger than 10 MiB are refused before reading; your current save stays unchanged.
  • Saving a backup also asks your browser to protect Pengyu’s saved data from automatic cleanup. Some browsers ask your permission first.

Reset / delete everything

  • Open Settings → Your data → Reset everything (also available in History), then choose Continue.
  • You can save a backup on the next screen. To finish deleting, choose Reset everything again. Choose Keep my data to cancel.
  • Reset removes your saved plans, workout history, Fish, unlocks, preferences and local action list from this browser, including leftover data from the old preview. It starts Pengyu fresh and cannot be undone without a backup.
  • Reset leaves the splash flag and offline app files in place. To remove those too, close Pengyu’s tabs and clear this site’s stored data in your browser settings. Reopening Pengyu starts a new save and can cache its files again.
  • Downloaded backup and calendar files remain where you saved them. Delete those separately, along with any calendar events you imported. Reset here does not delete copies in another browser.
