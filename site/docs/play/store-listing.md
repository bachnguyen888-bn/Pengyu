## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Unhealthy foods can be addicting. So why can’t healthy living be?

Pengyu makes your workout week something you want to come back to. One character, one job: plan → log → train moves → upgrade.

Plan
Pick 2, 3 or 4 workouts for the week. Choose the days, a time and a place. It takes about 30 seconds.

Log
Worked out? Tap Begin. Any workout, any length, counts. Pengu’s train rolls to the next station.

Earn Fish
Each planned workout pays 10 Fish. Finish your week for a 25 Fish bonus. Come back after a missed day and your next planned workout earns a Comeback stamp, worth 15 more.

Upgrade
Spend Fish on Pengu’s scarf, cap, backpack and conductor outfit. Add a headlamp, new colors or an extra car to the train. Workouts beyond your plan collect looks-only hats and decals. Prices never change, and what you unlock is yours for good.

Fair by design
• No ads.
• Nothing to buy. Only workouts earn Fish.
• No random rewards or loot boxes.
• No leaderboards and no streak guilt. A missed day never costs Fish or gear.
• One free cover each month keeps your week streak going.

Private by design
• No account to make.
• Your plan, workouts and Fish stay on your phone.
• Works offline after the first launch.
• Save a backup file any time.

Pengyu doesn’t measure anything about your body. It helps you plan your workouts and show up.
